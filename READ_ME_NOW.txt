# 🚀 YOUR A/B TESTING APP IS READY!

Since manual browser testing is required, follow these steps:

1. **Open Dashboard**: Go to http://localhost:8080/dashboard.html
2. **Create Experiment**:
   - Click "New Experiment"
   - Name: "My First Test"
   - Target URL: http://localhost:8080/index.html
   - Variant B URL: http://localhost:8080/variant-b.html
   - Click **Launch**
3. **Test It**:
   - Open http://localhost:8080/index.html in a new tab.
   - You will see either the White page or Dark Mode page.
   - Click "Sign Up" to track conversion.
   - Check "Stats" on the Dashboard.

Enjoy!
